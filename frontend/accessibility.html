Accessibility statement

Atelier Joinery Studio wants everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to design a wardrobe and request a quotation on this site. This statement describes what we have made accessible, how to use the site, what is not yet accessible, and how to reach us.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

How the site was made accessible

The site was adapted according to Israeli Standard 5568 (accessibility of web content), which is based on the W3C Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.0, at level AA. The adjustments include:

  • Every page can be used with a keyboard alone, and the keyboard focus is always clearly visible.
  • A “Skip to main content” link is the first item on every page.
  • Text and buttons meet the AA colour contrast ratio (at least 4.5:1 for normal text).
  • Form fields and buttons have names that screen readers announce; decorative icons are hidden from screen readers.
  • Pages declare their language and use headings and landmarks (header, main content, footer).
  • Images have text alternatives. The 3D wardrobe preview is described, and every value it shows (dimensions, bays, materials) is also written in the panel beside it.
  • Every drag-and-drop action in the interior editor also has a button or keyboard equivalent: select a component and use the arrow keys to move it, or Delete to remove it.
  • The design assistant can be used by typing as well as by voice, and no change is applied until you press a button to accept it.
  • The layout adapts to narrow screens and to browser zoom up to 200%.

How to use the site

  • Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between links, buttons and fields, and Enter or Space to activate them.
  • Enlarge text with your browser zoom (Ctrl++, or Cmd++ on Mac).
  • The site works with common screen readers, such as NVDA, JAWS and VoiceOver, in current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.

What is not yet accessible, and alternatives

  • The 3D preview itself cannot be explored with a screen reader. All of its information is available as text in the side panel and on the review page.
  • Voice conversation with the design assistant needs a microphone and hearing. The same assistant is available by typing.
  • Some sample screens in the staff area still contain example content that will be replaced.

If you cannot complete a design or a request because of an accessibility problem, contact us and we will help you complete it by phone or email.
